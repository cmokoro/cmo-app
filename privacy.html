Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

CMO Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used by one creator to manage their own YouTube channels, Dim Atlas and Side Quest Club. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions for the owner's own channels only:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos and thumbnails.
  • youtube.readonly: to read channel details and the owner's own video list, titles and upload or processing status.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read views, watch time and subscribers gained for the owner's own videos.

The app does not access data about viewers, commenters or any other channel, and it does not collect personal information from anyone other than the channel owner.

How the data is used

  • To upload and schedule videos that the owner has approved.
  • To avoid scheduling conflicts on the owner's channel.
  • To summarise the performance of the owner's own videos and plan future content.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens and analytics figures are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file and a local database. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the app.
  • The app does not sell, rent or share YouTube data with anyone. It does not use YouTube data for advertising.
  • When planning new videos, the app may send the titles and aggregate statistics of the owner's own videos to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to generate content ideas. No viewer data is included.
  • Analytics data is refreshed daily, and any analytics figures older than 30 days are automatically deleted from the local database.

Cookies

The app is a command-line program and does not use cookies or tracking. This website does not set cookies.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (Third-party apps and services). Revoking access stops the app from reaching the channel. The owner can delete all locally stored data by deleting the app's data and secrets folders.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
