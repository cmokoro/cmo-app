A private publishing tool for two YouTube channels

CMO Publisher is a command-line tool used by one creator to upload, schedule and measure videos on their own YouTube channels, Dim Atlas and Side Quest Club.

Not a public service. CMO Publisher has no sign-up and no other users. It runs on the channel owner's own computer and only ever signs in to the owner's own channels.

What it does

  • Uploads videos the owner has produced and personally approved, as private videos with a scheduled publish time, title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • Reads channel data (the owner's own video list and upload status) to avoid scheduling two videos in the same slot.
  • Reads YouTube Analytics for the owner's own videos (views, watch time, subscribers gained) to decide what to make next.

Which Google permissions it uses

  • youtube.upload: upload videos and set thumbnails on the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: read the owner's channel and video details.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read analytics reports for the owner's channel.

It cannot delete videos, change channel settings, post comments, or access any other channel.
